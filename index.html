Ask us anything ↗
How early should we book EVENTRA?

For weddings and larger events, reaching out several months ahead is ideal. For smaller celebrations, contact us and we'll discuss the available timeline.

Can you work with our budget?

Yes. We begin by understanding your priorities and budget, then suggest an event plan that makes the best use of them.

Do you handle event-day coordination?

Yes. Event-day coordination can be part of your plan, helping suppliers, schedules and details stay organized.

Can we customize a package?

Absolutely. Each celebration is different, so we can discuss a plan based on your needs and the kind of experience you want.
